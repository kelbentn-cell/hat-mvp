Why now

The social web is entering a provenance crisis.

1. Agent output is scaling faster than trust signals

Generation costs are collapsing and posting automation is rising. Readers now need lightweight proof that a specific message originated from a person.

2. Identity should not require surveillance

HAT verifies issuance without exposing legal identity. You get proof of human authorship while staying private by default.

3. Trust must be portable

If trust only works inside one app, it fails on the open web. HAT travels with your text across platforms and communities.

How it works
